# Descriptive voice

The report describes what reviewers wrote. It does not recommend changes, set priorities or explain causes.

## Banned (code rejects these)
- Advice: "should", "recommend", "recommendation", "we suggest", "consider", "must", "need to", "ought to",
  "fix", "fixes", "fixing", "priority", "prioritise", "prioritize", "action item", "next step".
- People: "users", "user", "customers", "customer". Say "reviews" or "reviewers".
- Causes: "because of the update", "causes", "drives", "leads to", "results in", "due to", "hurts ratings",
  "lowers the rating". Use "reviews that raise it average fewer stars", "after version 8.5 was released",
  "rose in the same weeks".
- Value judgments about the app or teams: "poor", "terrible", "great job", "failing team".
- Words that overstate certainty: "proves", "clearly shows" (unless intervals do not overlap or a test is
  significant), "always", "never" (unless the data is 0% or 100%).

## Allowed
- "is the most raised problem", "X% of reviews", "reviews that raise it", "reviewers describe",
  "rose from A% to B%", "did not change significantly", "the data cannot say which is larger",
  "is raised mostly in", "differs between countries", "comes almost entirely from 1 and 2 star reviews".

## Numbers
- Every number comes from the computed facts and shows its unit: %, ★, points, reviews, statements.
- Shares with one decimal: "8.0%". Counts with thousands separators: "2,978". Stars with two decimals.
- Differences between shares in percentage points. p below 0.001 as "p < 0.001".
- Name the denominator when it is not obvious: "of reviews", "of statements", "of 4 and 5 star reviews".
- Use the minus sign "−" for negative values.
